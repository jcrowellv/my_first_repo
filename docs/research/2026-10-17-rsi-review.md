## Decisions

- **Separate experiment selection from agenda setting.** [TasteVal](https://pzeroresearch.com/work/tasteval/) challenges the claim that experiment choice is uniformly a human bottleneck. Its supplied objectives and selected tasks do not settle broad program-level judgment.
- **Treat local optimization as a component of RSI.** The [Vals index](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/rsi_index) measures several bounded research tasks. A benchmark trend or threshold is not a calibrated arrival distribution and does not demonstrate repeated successor cycles.
- **Retain scientific verification boundaries.** The [OpenAI mathematics release](https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/), [3SUM/APSP preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06783v1), [KLS preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05474v1) and [TheoPhysEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16733v1) support different kinds of progress. Human contribution, formal checking, final-answer grading and expert review remain distinct.
- **Correct stronger screenshot interpretations.** [Materials calculations](https://github.com/spicylemonade/compensated-magnet-ledger) await relevant physical tests. [ECDSA seed reconstruction](https://www.anthropic.com/research/ecdsa-curve-origins) does not recover private keys. [Goodfire's interpretability essay](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/) offers a working hypothesis rather than an established alignment solution. The dossier qualifies each claim at its source.
- **Include evidence beyond the packet.** [METR's Opus 5.5 assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-09-22-claude-opus-5-5/) adds bounded evaluation results and a separately labeled preliminary acceleration estimate. Its [October causal-assessment proposal](https://metr.org/blog/2026-10-14-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment-proposal/) explains why compute, data, labor and internal-model access must be accounted for. A proposal is not a completed measurement.
- **Preserve numerical history.** All 43 forecast records and the July capability rubric remain unchanged. October updates the qualitative assessment, bottlenecks and review notes. New distributions would require a documented calibration decision and appended records.

## What would change the conclusion

The new structural monitor asks for at least two successive improvement cycles, with the resulting researcher evaluated on fresh held-out tasks. It requires an account of human steering, compute, data and failed attempts. A capability gain attributable only to extra resources, repeated benchmark tuning or a stronger external model does not resolve it. No deadline has been invented.

The five-stage explorer is an editorial decomposition—execution, experiment selection, verification, agenda setting and successor improvement. Its labels are neither probabilities nor a capability completion score.
